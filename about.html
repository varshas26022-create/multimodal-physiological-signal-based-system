About Muscle Cramps

What is a Muscle Cramp?

A muscle cramp is a sudden, involuntary contraction of a muscle.

Common Causes:

  • Muscle fatigue
  • Dehydration
  • Electrolyte imbalance
  • Overexertion

Why EMG + GSR + Flex?

EMG detects muscle activity, GSR measures skin conductivity (stress response), and Flex sensor detects muscle contraction level.
